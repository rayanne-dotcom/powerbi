Case 2 - Sales performance report
I used a sample dataset and AI to generate answers to the following questions: 
What is the annual sales performance? 
→ Answered by the "Annual Sales" KPI (showing the consolidated yearly total). 

What is the monthly performance, and which month is the best? 
→ Answered by the "Best Month Name" KPI (e.g., August) and the "Monthly Sales" chart. 

What is the Year-over-Year (YoY) growth? 
→ Answered by the "YoY Growth %" and "YoY Growth Month %" KPIs, which show whether there was an increase or decrease compared to the previous year. 

Which stores have the highest sales volume? 
→ Answered by the "Store’s Ranking" table, listing cities and sales figures. 

Where are the stores located? 
→ Answered by the "Store’s Location" map, showing the geographic distribution.
